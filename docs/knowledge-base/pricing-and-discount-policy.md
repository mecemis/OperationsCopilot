# Aurora Supply Co. Pricing and Discount Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Commercial Finance. Confidential — internal use only.

## Margin Floors

No order may be priced below the category margin floor without written approval.

| Category | Target gross margin | Absolute floor |
|----------|--------------------|----------------|
| Power Tools | 32% | 22% |
| Electronics | 28% | 18% |
| Safety Equipment | 40% | 30% |
| Hand Tools | 45% | 33% |
| Consumables | 50% | 38% |

Margin is calculated on landed cost, which includes purchase price, inbound freight, and duty.
Quoting on purchase price alone systematically overstates margin by three to six points and is a
recurring audit finding.

## Discount Approval Matrix

| Discount off list | Approver |
|-------------------|----------|
| Up to 5% | Sales representative |
| 5% to 12% | Regional sales manager |
| 12% to 20% | Commercial Finance |
| Above 20%, or below the margin floor | Commercial Director and Operations Director jointly |

Approvals must be recorded against the quote before the order is confirmed. Retrospective
approval is not permitted.

## Volume Tiers

Standard volume breaks apply automatically and do not consume discount authority:

- 25 to 99 units: 3% off list
- 100 to 499 units: 7% off list
- 500 units or more: 12% off list

Volume breaks stack with a negotiated discount only up to a combined 20% off list. Beyond that,
the joint approval rule applies.

## Price Changes

List prices are reviewed quarterly. Increases require 30 days' notice to customers on a framework
agreement and take effect on the first day of the following month. Orders already confirmed are
honored at the price in effect on the confirmation date.

Cost increases above 8% from a supplier may trigger an out-of-cycle price review, which requires
Commercial Director approval and evidence of the supplier cost movement.

## Discontinued and Clearance Pricing

Discontinued stock may be discounted up to 40% off list without regard to the margin floor,
because the alternative is a full write-off against the obsolescence provision. Clearance pricing
must be applied at the SKU level and cannot be combined with volume breaks.
