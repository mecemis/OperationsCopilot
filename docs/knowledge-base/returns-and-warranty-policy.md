# Aurora Supply Co. Returns and Warranty Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Customer Operations. Applies to all regions.

## Standard Return Window

Customers may return unopened goods within 30 calendar days of delivery for a full refund.
Opened goods in resalable condition may be returned within 30 days subject to a 15% restocking
fee. After 30 days, returns are accepted only under warranty.

Business customers on a framework agreement have a 60-day return window and are exempt from the
restocking fee on their first two returns per quarter.

## Return Merchandise Authorization

Every return requires an RMA number issued before the goods are shipped back. Returns received
without an RMA are held for 14 days pending identification and then returned to sender at the
customer's cost.

The RMA process is:

1. Customer raises a return request with order number and reason code.
2. Customer Operations validates eligibility against the return window and product condition.
3. An RMA number and prepaid label are issued, valid for 21 days.
4. The warehouse inspects the returned goods within 3 business days of receipt.
5. Refund or replacement is issued within 5 business days of a passed inspection.

## Warranty Terms by Category

Warranty runs from the delivery date, not the order date.

| Category | Warranty period | Covers |
|----------|-----------------|--------|
| Power Tools | 24 months | Manufacturing defects, motor failure, and premature bearing wear |
| Electronics | 12 months | Component failure under normal use |
| Safety Equipment | 36 months | Any defect affecting protective function |
| Hand Tools | Lifetime | Material and workmanship defects |
| Consumables | None | Not covered; sold as-is |

Warranty does not cover cosmetic damage, wear consistent with normal use, damage from misuse or
unauthorized modification, or products with a removed or defaced serial number.

## Warranty Claims on Discontinued Products

Discontinued products keep their full warranty period. When no replacement unit is available,
Customer Operations offers, in order of preference: the nearest current-generation equivalent at
no additional cost, a credit note for the original purchase price, or a cash refund.

## Faulty on Arrival

Goods reported faulty within 72 hours of delivery are handled as faulty on arrival: a replacement
ships immediately without waiting for the return to be received or inspected, and no restocking
fee applies. Faulty-on-arrival rates above 1% for a supplier trigger a quality review under the
supplier management standard.
