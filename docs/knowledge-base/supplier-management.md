# Aurora Supply Co. Supplier Management Standard

Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Procurement. Review cadence: twice yearly.

## Supplier Tiers

Suppliers are graded into three tiers based on on-time delivery rate, quality acceptance rate,
and commercial importance.

- **Tier 1** — strategic partners. On-time delivery above 95%, quality acceptance above 99%.
  Standard lead time 10 days. Eligible for consignment stock and joint forecasting.
- **Tier 2** — approved suppliers. On-time delivery 85% to 95%. Standard lead time 21 days.
  Require a purchase order for every shipment.
- **Tier 3** — transactional or single-source suppliers. On-time delivery below 85%, or a sole
  source with no qualified alternative. Standard lead time 35 days. Require dual sign-off on
  orders above 10,000.

Tier is reviewed every six months. Two consecutive quarters below tier thresholds triggers an
automatic downgrade and a documented remediation plan.

## Lead Times and Expediting

Planning uses the standard lead time for the supplier's tier unless a contract specifies
otherwise. Expedited orders carry a surcharge of 15% to 30% and must be approved by the
Operations Director. Expediting is justified only when the shortfall would otherwise cause a
customer-facing stockout that cannot be covered by an inter-warehouse transfer.

## Single-Source Risk

Any product available from exactly one supplier is flagged as single-source and carries double
the normal safety stock. Procurement maintains a qualification pipeline with a target of at
least one alternate supplier per category within twelve months.

## Supplier Performance Reviews

Tier 1 suppliers are reviewed quarterly with a scorecard covering delivery, quality, cost
movement, and responsiveness. Tier 2 and Tier 3 suppliers are reviewed annually. Scorecards are
shared with the supplier within ten business days of the review.

A supplier whose quality acceptance rate falls below 97% is placed on enhanced inspection at the
supplier's cost until three consecutive shipments pass without a defect finding.
